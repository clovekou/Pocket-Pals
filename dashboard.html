The dashboard needs the Pocket Pal server running on your computer.

  1. On your Mac, open the PocketPal folder and double-click Start Pocket Pal.command
    (or open Terminal in that folder and run python3 server.py)
  2. Your browser opens the dashboard at http://localhost:8000/dashboard.html.
  3. After uploading or changing things, push to GitHub to update the online game.

Upload sprites

Use a PMD SpriteCollab sprite folder, the same layout as the ones you already have (sprite/ with AnimData.xml and the -Anim.png sheets; sprite/0000/Shiny and portrait/ are optional). You can pick the folder, a .zip of it, or drag either onto the box. Uploading a Dex number that already exists replaces its sprites.

Backgrounds

Upload your own scene pictures (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP, up to 15 MB each), then choose where each one is used: everywhere, or per landscape. The Pokémon stands near the bottom of the picture, so images with ground along the bottom look best; tall images (around 1080 × 1600) fit phones best.

Evolution lines

How your Pokémon evolve right now. A Pokémon with more than one evolution goes toward the branch you haven't raised yet. Uses the search below.

Pokémon

Set each one's type, what it evolves into and at what level, its egg rarity and its battle stats. Press Save changes at the bottom when you're done; the game uses the new settings next time it's opened or refreshed.
