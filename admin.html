Explore maps

Choose which Pokémon live on each map, its legendary and its main chase, the Pokémon players hunt there. Changes go live for every player as soon as you save.

Evolutions

Every evolution family in the game and what each step needs: a level or an evolution item, and optionally a time of day. Press ✎ on any Pokémon (or pick one below) to change how it evolves. Changes reach every player's game straight away.

Times use each player's own clock: morning 5:00–11:59, afternoon 12:00–17:59, night 18:00–4:59. "Hold" items are given to the Pokémon and work at its next level-up; other items are used on it straight away. Friendship evolves it once its bond (0–100, up to +20 a day) reaches the number. No rows = it doesn't evolve.

Backgrounds

Add backgrounds players can buy once with Pokécoins in Options → Backgrounds, then switch to any time. Tall pictures work best (phones are about 9 × 19). Pictures are shrunk to fit and saved in Firestore.

Delete player

Removes their profile, save, username, player ID, widget, friends, friend requests, trades and battle records, and frees their username. Their game also wipes its own copy of the save the next time it's online. This can't be undone.
